v1.11 title-weight check (stem width / glyph height, NOT ink coverage)
Method: PIL binarize (luminance < 180); glyph groups = column runs with any
dark pixel; stem = consecutive columns from the stem edge with dark-core
coverage >= 0.6 over the glyph band. Same rule for render, probe, original.
ASCII art was printed per glyph during measurement and eyeballed.
